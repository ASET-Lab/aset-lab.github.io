Michael Foster and Neil Walkinshaw will be spending the next year working with partners in the Environment Agency to apply the outcomes from their [Causal Testing Framework](https://github.com/CITCOM-project/CausalTestingFramework) to hydrology models used to model droughts and floods by the [Environment Agency](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/environment-agency).
